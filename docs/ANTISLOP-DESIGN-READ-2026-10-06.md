# Anti-Slop Design Read: Dashboard Comercial

Reading this as an internal B2B commercial operations dashboard for executives, national and unit management, coordinators, and sales advisors. It should feel precise, calm, and recognizable as CCV, with compact data views and clear state changes.

**Dials:** ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Design decisions

- **Green is the operating color.** It marks primary actions, active navigation, and positive performance because those states align with the CCV identity.
- **Amber signals attention.** Keep it for warnings and secondary emphasis; financial chart categories retain distinct colors so units remain distinguishable.
- **Inter and tabular numerals support scanning.** The user requested Inter for dense UI and stable financial columns; larger KPI values can use the existing display face.
- **Icons identify real destinations and states.** Keep the existing icon vocabulary for navigation, alerts, and chart actions; avoid decorative icon badges.
- **The login grid is a brand motif.** The user requested the cyber grid adapted to CCV; keep it confined to the login panel and disable its motion for reduced-motion preferences.
- **Cards use restrained borders and shadows.** Their job is to group independent measures and tables, not to make every surface float.
- **Motion stays subtle.** Use short interaction feedback and real loading/status motion only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Keep the application’s own language and data.** Do not add marketing claims, sample customers, fabricated metrics, or destinations that the role cannot open.
